Install it in six simple steps.

Keep this page open while you install. macOS may ask you to approve this independently distributed app the first time it opens.

  1. Download the installer

    All done! If it didn’t work, click here for a direct download.

    Starting your download…

  2. Launch the installer

    Double-click EchoDesk-Desktop-v0.2.7-macos-universal.dmg in Downloads.

  3. Drag EchoDesk Desktop to Applications

    In the installer window, drag EchoDesk Desktop from the left onto Applications on the right.

  4. Open the app from Applications

    Open Finder, select Applications, then double-click EchoDesk Desktop.

  5. Allow the app to launch

    macOS may initially block the app because EchoDesk is independently distributed and does not currently use Apple’s paid Developer Program.

    System Settings›Privacy & Security›Security›Open Anyway›Open Anyway

    Before approving it: verify the downloaded DMG against the SHA-256 value on the download page. You may be asked for Touch ID or your Mac password.

  6. Launch EchoDesk Desktop

    Open EchoDesk Desktop and follow the welcome prompts to set up your first EchoDesk.
