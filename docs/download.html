Up and running

Three steps. One desk upgrade.

  1. 01

    Install

    Run the Windows installer, or open the Mac disk image and drag EchoDesk Desktop to Applications. These independent builds may show a platform security prompt.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Plug EchoDesk Mini into your computer. USB is the normal connection and remains available for recovery and firmware updates.

  3. 03

    Make it yours

    Follow guided setup, then choose your utilities, appearance and optional Wi-Fi connection.
